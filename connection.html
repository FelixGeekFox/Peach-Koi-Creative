What membership means

Independent voices.
Shared standards.

The Creative Collective brings together communities with a creative, welcoming spirit. Membership is a connection between independent spaces, not a merger or a transfer of ownership.

Keep your identity

Your community keeps its name, creative direction, platforms and moderation team. Its own rules continue to apply alongside the collective’s shared standards.

Be part of the picture

Approved members can be featured in our community directory and use the collective membership badge. Help visitors discover the wider collective through a link where practical.

Grow through connection

Explore shared prompts, events, resources and collaborations when they suit your community. Participation should be intentional, not an obligation to join every activity.

Membership does not mean PKC owns your content, manages your platform or endorses every post. Applications are reviewed; sending one does not automatically grant membership or permission to use the badge.

✦ Our collective standards

Room to create.
Responsibility to care.

  1. Respect people and identities

    Welcome different identities and experiences. No hate speech, targeted harassment, discriminatory exclusion or deliberate misgendering.

  2. Protect privacy and consent

    No doxxing or sharing private conversations, personal information or someone’s likeness without permission. Do not pressure people to disclose more than they want to.

  3. Create responsibly

    Respect creators’ rights, credit work appropriately and be honest about AI use when it matters. Do not present someone else’s work or identity as your own.

  4. Moderate with care

    Keep clear community rules and an accessible route for reporting concerns. Respond to harmful behaviour rather than allowing it to become the culture.

  5. Make boundaries clear

    Follow the law and your platform’s rules. Clearly communicate content boundaries and age restrictions where applicable. Never permit exploitative or non-consensual sexual content.

  6. Connect without coercion

    No brigading, spam campaigns or pressure to join, buy or participate. Cross-promotion and collaborations should respect each community’s rules and consent.

  7. Represent membership honestly

    Use the membership badge only after approval. Keep your listing and moderator contact details current, and don’t imply you speak for the whole collective.

  8. Stay accountable

    Raise collective concerns privately with the moderators. Membership or listings may be paused or removed if these standards are not upheld. Communities can also request to leave.
