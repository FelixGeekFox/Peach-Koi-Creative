✦ Keep the human part

Create with curiosity.
Share with care.

Give it a clear brief

Describe the goal, audience, format and constraints. Change one thing at a time, compare results, and save the choices that actually helped.

Protect people’s privacy

Don’t upload private conversations, personal information or someone else’s likeness without permission. Read how a tool stores and uses your inputs.

Keep your judgement

Verify factual claims, respect creators’ rights and disclose AI use where it matters. Your taste, editing and responsibility still shape the final work.
